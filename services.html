Capabilities

What we deliver

Every line below is delivered by EWI staff or by a principal we directly represent. Nothing here is brokered.

Project sales & supply

Bill-of-quantity pricing, technical submittals for consultant approval, and staged delivery aligned to your construction programme.

Turnkey lab fit-out

Layout development, casework, fume extraction, services coordination and instrumentation delivered as a single package with one handover.

Lab furniture & fume hoods

Benching, storage, safety cabinets, fume hoods and glove boxes specified to the chemistry and the airflow, not to a brochure page.

Lab automation

Sample handling, prep and throughput automation for laboratories under rising workload without rising headcount.

Scientific instrumentation

Analytical, thermal, mechanical testing and materials characterisation instruments, with application support behind the selection.

Medical & healthcare equipment

Clinical and diagnostic laboratory equipment and consumables for hospitals, clinics and public health laboratories.

Electrical materials supply

Conduit, fittings, terminations, labelling, hose and tooling held in Doha for projects, construction and maintenance works.

Cable management & enclosures

Tray, trunking, conduit systems and IP-rated enclosures selected for the environment they will actually sit in.

Installation & commissioning

Site works, installation qualification, validation and handover documentation carried out by our own technical engineers.

Calibration & maintenance contracts

Annual service agreements, preventive schedules, calibration cycles and spare-part planning for your installed base.

Cyber professional services

Design, deployment, tuning, SIEM integration and operator knowledge transfer for network visibility and detection platforms.

Product training

Operator and maintainer training delivered with the principal, on your equipment, in your facility.

Process

How an EWI job runs

Step 01

Scope & review

We read the specification, drawing or bill of quantities and come back with questions before we come back with a price.

Step 02

Quote & submittal

Scoped quotation with compliance statements and the technical submittal your consultant needs to approve it.

Step 03

Supply & install

Staged delivery from local stock or factory order, then installation and commissioning by our engineers.

Step 04

Support & maintain

Training, warranty administration, calibration cycles and a maintenance contract if you want the uptime guaranteed.
