Engineering Services That Support Your Project from Start to Finish

We do not simply drop boxes at your gate. Our team works with you to verify specifications, manage delivery, install equipment, and provide maintenance long after the initial handover.

Capabilities

What We Deliver

Every service below is carried out by our own staff or in direct partnership with manufacturers we represent in Qatar.

Project sales and quotations

We review your bills of quantities, prepare itemized quotations, supply compliant technical datasheets, and organize staged deliveries.

Turnkey laboratory fit out

We coordinate room planning and MEP services, install laboratory casework, fume extraction hoods, and scientific instruments.

Laboratory furniture and fume hoods

We help select work surfaces and extraction systems suited to the specific chemicals, heat, and safety requirements of your work.

Laboratory automation

Automated sample preparation and fluid handling machinery to keep testing workflows fast and consistent under high sample volumes.

Scientific instrumentation supply

We supply spectrometers, thermal analysis units, mechanical testers, and general lab equipment from international manufacturers.

Medical and diagnostic equipment

We deliver diagnostic instruments, specialized storage refrigerators, lab consumables, and testing kits to medical clinics and hospitals.

Electrical site materials supply

We supply conduit, cable trays, junction boxes, wire connectors, and marking labels from our local warehouse in Doha.

Cable management and enclosures

We supply cable trays, trunking, metal conduit, and weatherproof IP rated junction boxes chosen specifically for site conditions in Qatar.

Structured cabling and data centre

We supply and install copper cables, fibre backbones, server racks, network switches, and modular server enclosures.

Installation, testing, and commissioning

Our engineers install equipment on site, run operational verification tests, complete IQ/OQ validation, and deliver signed handover reports.

Calibration and preventive maintenance

We perform routine inspections, recalibrate instruments against certified standards, clean assemblies, and replace consumable parts.

Cyber security professional services

We place network TAPs at key switch locations, aggregate traffic through packet brokers, and link threat detection alerts into your SOC.

Equipment training for operators

We train your team on daily operation, basic maintenance checks, safety procedures, and routine troubleshooting.

Our process

How We Execute Your Order

Step 01

Review your scope

You send us your bill of quantities, drawings, or equipment list. Our engineers review the technical requirements and ask any necessary clarifying questions before quoting.

Step 02

Quotation and submittal

We issue a detailed commercial quotation alongside manufacturer datasheets, compliance statements, and test certificates for consultant approval.

Step 03

Delivery and installation

We deliver materials to your site from local stock or factory order, followed by on site assembly, connection, and testing by our engineers.

Step 04

Handover and ongoing support

We provide testing certificates, user training, warranty documentation, and optional maintenance contracts to keep your facility operating reliably.
