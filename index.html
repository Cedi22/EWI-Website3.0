Who we are

A Doha Trading and Engineering Supplier You Can Deal With Directly

East West Trading International began in Doha in 2002. We started by building specialized laboratories and expanded our business to support electrical contracting, commercial data networks, and network security.

We do not work as a catalog broker. We keep our own sales and service engineers on staff. The same engineer who reviews your tender specifications will help coordinate delivery and support your team on site.

  • Local warehouse in Doha with materials ready for delivery to your site
  • Approved supplier for government authorities, energy firms, and major contractors
  • Direct authorized agent for international manufacturers
  • In house engineers for installation, testing, calibration, and training

What we do

From Material Supply to Complete Installation

We handle technical procurement from the first bill of quantities review through to site commissioning and annual maintenance.

Material supply and tendering

We quote directly from your bill of quantities, prepare technical submittals for consultant approval, and schedule deliveries to match your construction schedule.

Turnkey laboratory fit out

We supply the complete laboratory package, including space planning, fume hoods, benching, gas lines, and specialized test equipment.

Site installation and commissioning

Our technicians handle site installation, verify connections, conduct qualification tests, and complete formal handover paperwork.

Maintenance and calibration

We provide scheduled preventive maintenance, equipment calibration, and spare parts support to keep your facilities running without stoppage.

Electrical site materials

We supply conduit, fittings, industrial enclosures, terminal connectors, and labeling tools straight from our warehouse in Doha.

Structured cabling and server rooms

We provide copper network cables, fibre backbones, patch panels, and modular server room setups with BDCOM and FABNET equipment.

Cyber security deployment

Our engineers configure network traffic taps and threat detection tools, connect them with your monitoring system, and train your staff.
