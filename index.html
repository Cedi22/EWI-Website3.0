Who we are

A local supplier that behaves like a principal

East West Trading International is a Qatari establishment serving the scientific, industrial and construction communities in the State of Qatar. We came to the market with turnkey laboratory expertise and grew into electrical materials distribution, network & data centre infrastructure and, most recently, cyber security.

We are deliberately staffed with high-calibre specialists rather than resellers-by-catalogue. Sales engineers scope the requirement, technical engineers commission it, and the same team answers the phone afterwards.

  • Stock held in Doha for projects, construction and maintenance works
  • Approved supplier to government, oil & gas and semi-government bodies
  • Sole and authorised representation for world-renowned manufacturers
  • Installation, commissioning, calibration and training under one contract
