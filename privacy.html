Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

East West Trading International W.L.L. (EWI) is committed to protecting your personal data in full compliance with the State of Qatar Law No. 13 of 2016 Concerning Personal Data Privacy Protection.

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

This privacy notice applies to all personal data collected and processed by:

2. Scope & Legal Framework

We process personal data strictly in compliance with Qatar Law No. 13 of 2016 (*Promulgating the Protection of the Privacy of Personal Data*). We adhere to the fundamental principles of data minimization, lawful processing, transparency, accuracy, and robust technical security.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to fulfill commercial inquiries, engineering tenders, and procurement services:

  • Contact Details: Name, corporate email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, company or government ministry name, and job title when voluntarily submitted via our contact form, email, or telephone.
  • Technical Project Details: Bill of Quantities (BOQs), architectural drawings, product specifications, and division preferences provided during project inquiries.
  • Technical Server Logs: Standard, non-identifying web server access logs (IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, timestamp) recorded automatically for security and server health monitoring.

Notice on Cookies & Tracking: This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party behavioral advertising scripts. All icon fonts and visual libraries are self-hosted locally on our server.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data exclusively for legitimate commercial and engineering purposes:

  • To respond to quotation requests (RFQs), technical inquiries, and project consultation requests.
  • To execute commercial supply agreements, equipment installation, testing, and commissioning in Qatar.
  • To maintain warranty, maintenance, and calibration records for delivered lab and electrical equipment.
  • To comply with statutory financial, customs, and legal reporting obligations in the State of Qatar.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

EWI strictly maintains data confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data to any third party.

Personal data is shared only with authorized principal manufacturers (such as Thermo Fisher Scientific, BDCOM, NEOX Networks, or Stamus Networks) strictly when required for equipment warranty registration, factory certification, or software license provisioning.

6. Data Security & Retention

We maintain strict administrative, organizational, and technical security safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or accidental loss. Personal data is retained only for the duration required to fulfill project and warranty commitments, or as required by Qatar commercial and taxation laws.

7. Your Rights Under Qatar PDPPL

Under Qatar Law No. 13 of 2016, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request confirmation of whether your data is being processed and obtain a copy.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data when no longer needed for commercial or statutory purposes.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing where applicable under statutory guidelines.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our data compliance desk at eastwest@qatar.net.qa. We will respond promptly within statutory timelines.
