Two decades of getting technical scopes delivered in Qatar

East West Trading International was established in Doha in 2002 to serve the scientific community of the State of Qatar. Three divisions later, the method has not changed: represent good principals properly, keep engineers on staff, and hold stock where the project is.

Our story

From turnkey laboratories to three divisions

EWI began with turnkey laboratory solutions — the kind of long-term project where casework, fume extraction, services and instrumentation all have to arrive in the right order and work together on handover. That work built a large installed base of advanced scientific instrumentation across Qatar’s universities, hospitals, research institutes and national energy companies.

The Electrical Division followed the same customers onto their construction and maintenance sites, supplying cable management, enclosures, terminations, identification and tooling from stock in Doha. The Cyber Security Division is our newest: network visibility and threat detection, brought in with two specialist principals and delivered with professional services rather than sold as a box.

Across all three, we remain a customer-driven local services organisation with a deliberate policy of heavy investment in staff of high calibre and specialisation.

How we work

Three commitments we hold to on every order

Represent, don’t just resell

We take authorised representation seriously: factory training, genuine product, valid warranty, and a principal we can escalate to when a project needs it.

Keep engineers on staff

Specification review, submittals, installation, commissioning and after-sales all sit with our own sales and technical engineers — not a subcontractor you never meet.

Hold stock in Doha

Material on the doorstep for projects, construction and maintenance works, so a shortage on site does not become a six-week import.
