Supplying Engineering and Laboratory Projects in Qatar Since 2002

East West Trading International is a Qatari trading and engineering establishment located in Doha. For over two decades, we have supplied technical products, laboratory systems, and site materials backed by local stock and our own engineering team.

Our background

How We Grew From Laboratory Fit Outs to Four Divisions

We started EWI in 2002 with a focus on complete laboratory installations. That work required managing detailed scopes where benching, fume extraction, gas piping, and sensitive scientific instruments all had to fit together and pass strict inspection. Through those projects, we built lasting relationships with universities, hospitals, research centres, and oil and gas companies throughout Qatar.

As our clients took on new facilities, they needed reliable sources for site materials. We launched our Electrical Division to supply cable management, enclosures, and tools directly to job sites. Later, we added our Network & Data Centre Division to supply structured cabling, fibre, and modular server rooms. Our Cyber Security Division followed, providing local engineering services for network traffic capture and threat monitoring.

Today, we continue to run our business the same way: we keep knowledgeable engineers on our payroll, we hold materials locally in Doha, and we take full responsibility for what we sell.

What we offer

Solutions & Engineering Capabilities

Detailed technical scope, manufacturer backing, and execution workflows across our primary operational sectors in Qatar.

Lab Systems

Turnkey laboratory design, fume extraction hoods, scientific casework, and analytical testing instruments.

We deliver complete laboratory packages from initial room layout and MEP coordination to final qualification. Our solutions include specialized chemical fume hoods, laminar flow units, steel casework, and precision analytical equipment.

Every installation is executed by our Doha-based engineers under strict international safety and quality standards, supported by global manufacturing principals including Thermo Fisher Scientific, Kewaunee, and DURAN.

Electrical Supplies

Local stock of cable management trays, weatherproof enclosures, terminal blocks, and job-site tooling.

We maintain comprehensive inventory at our Doha warehouse for immediate dispatch to construction sites and industrial facilities. Our portfolio covers conduit fittings, heavy-duty cable trays, industrial junction boxes, and terminal connectors.

We represent leading brands such as Atkore, Weidmüller, Hensel, and Brady, ensuring certified materials with full submittal support for consultant approvals.

Network Infrastructure

Structured copper, optical fibre cabling, enterprise switches, and modular server room installations.

We supply and commission end-to-end communication backbones for corporate offices, healthcare facilities, and data centres across Qatar. Scope includes Cat6A/Cat7 copper cabling, OM3/OM4/OS2 optical fibre, patch panels, and server racks.

Partnered directly with BDCOM and FABNET to deliver enterprise switching, routing, and integrated micro data centre solutions built for continuous uptime.

Cyber Security

Network traffic capture hardware, packet brokers, and threat detection platforms with local engineering support.

We equip enterprise and critical infrastructure defense teams with high-speed packet brokers and network TAP systems. Our engineers deploy and integrate platforms like NEOX Networks and Stamus Networks into existing SOC environments.

We provide full installation, TAP configuration, traffic optimization, and staff operational onboarding with local technical support based in Doha.

Site Installation

Qualified technicians handling mechanical setup, equipment calibration, and formal handover paperwork.

Our in-house technical crew manages all physical site works, utility hookups, and system integrations. We liaise directly with main contractors, MEP teams, and client representatives to maintain schedule alignment.

Every installation concludes with rigorous verification testing, site commissioning reports, and official vendor handover documentation.

Maintenance & Calibration

Scheduled preventive servicing, certified instrument calibration, and genuine replacement parts support.

We provide structured annual maintenance contracts (AMC), periodic instrument re-calibration, and emergency site troubleshooting. Our service engineers maintain rapid response capabilities across Doha.

We stock critical spare parts and manufacturer consumables locally to guarantee operational continuity and avoid lengthy international shipment delays.
