Division 01

Lab Systems

Turnkey projects Instrumentation Furniture & fume hoods Automation

Our Lab Systems division handles the design, supply, and installation of complete laboratories. We work on new construction projects, lab renovations, and equipment upgrades for educational, medical, and industrial clients.

We work from the room layout forward: casework and fume extraction, MEP utility coordination, instrument selection, site installation, qualification testing, and long term maintenance.

  • Complete laboratory layout design and casework installation
  • Analytical testing, materials testing, and thermal analysis equipment
  • Chemical fume hoods, laminar flow cabinets, and safety storage
  • Automated sample handling and preparation equipment
  • Medical laboratory diagnostic devices and consumables
  • Glassware, general lab supplies, and replacement parts from stock

Division 02

Electrical Division

Cable management Enclosures Identification Site tooling

Our Electrical Division supplies essential installation materials and tooling to contractors, plant operators, and maintenance teams. We focus on materials needed every day on construction sites and plant turnarounds.

We quote against bills of quantity, prepare submittals for consultant approval, and phase delivery to your construction schedule rather than dropping the whole order at once.

  • Metallic and non metallic conduit, fittings, and cable trays
  • Weatherproof junction boxes, distribution enclosures, and cabinets
  • Terminal blocks, power distribution blocks, and connectors
  • Industrial wire markers, safety tags, and label printers
  • High voltage electrical test equipment
  • Industrial hoses, pneumatic fittings, and hand installation tools

Division 03

Network & Data Centre

Structured cabling Fibre optic Switching DCIM & AIM

Our Network & Data Centre division supplies physical connectivity and active network equipment for commercial buildings, industrial sites, and server facilities.

We work from the network design forward: containment pathways, copper and fibre cabling, active switches, access control, server racks, and modular data centre rooms.

  • Copper patch cords, Cat6 and Cat6A cables, and patch panels
  • Fibre optic cables, termination boxes, and optical connectors
  • Managed network switches, wireless access points, and routers
  • Modular and containerized data centre units
  • Server racks, cable organizers, and containment aisles
  • Environmental monitoring and access control for server rooms

Division 04

Cyber Security

Network visibility Threat detection Professional services

Our Cyber Security division focuses on network traffic monitoring and detection. We supply hardware and software that give your security team full visibility into network traffic without interrupting operations.

Our engineers design the traffic capture layer with network TAPs and packet brokers, install threat detection platforms, integrate alerts into your existing monitoring tools, and train your security team.

  • Network TAPs and packet brokers for capturing live traffic
  • Network detection and response platforms from Stamus Networks
  • Traffic monitoring tools for corporate IT and industrial OT networks
  • Integration with existing SIEM and security dashboards
  • Local configuration, system testing, and operator handover
