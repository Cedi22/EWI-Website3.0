Division 01

Lab Systems

Turnkey projects Instrumentation Furniture & fume hoods Automation

Our founding division. We came to the Qatari market with experience in turnkey laboratory solutions and took the lead on long-term laboratory projects — delivering a vast installed base of advanced scientific instrumentation from world-renowned manufacturers.

We work from the layout drawing forward: casework and fume extraction, services coordination, instrument selection, installation, qualification and the maintenance contract that keeps it all calibrated.

  • Turnkey laboratory design, supply and fit-out
  • Analytical, thermal, mechanical testing and materials instrumentation
  • Laboratory furniture, fume hoods and glove boxes
  • Lab automation and sample-handling systems
  • Medical and healthcare laboratory equipment
  • Glassware, consumables and spare parts from stock

Division 02

Electrical Division

Cable management Enclosures Identification Site tooling

Built to serve the contractors, operators and maintenance departments already working with us. The Electrical Division supplies the material that keeps a site moving — conduit and fittings, enclosures and terminations, labelling systems, hose and hand tools — from stock in Doha.

We quote against bills of quantity, prepare submittals for consultant approval, and phase delivery to the construction programme rather than dropping the whole package at once.

  • Conduit, fittings, cable tray and cable management systems
  • Enclosures, junction boxes and distribution housings
  • Terminal blocks, connectivity and industrial interfaces
  • Labelling, marking and identification systems
  • High-voltage test equipment
  • Pneumatic hose, metal hose and installation tooling

Division 03

Network & Data Centre

Structured cabling Fibre optic Switching DCIM & AIM

Our infrastructure layer. The Network & Data Centre Division supplies and installs the structured cabling, fibre optic backbone, switching and access systems that everything else on a project runs over — plus the modular data centre infrastructure that houses it.

We work from the network design forward: pathway and containment, copper and fibre cabling, active switching and access control, then rack, containment and DCIM/AIM systems for the data centre itself, commissioned and documented for handover.

  • Structured cabling: copper, fibre optic and containment systems
  • Network switching, access points and active infrastructure
  • Modular and containerised data centre infrastructure
  • Data centre racks, containment and cable management
  • DCIM and AIM (asset and infrastructure management) systems
  • Access control and physical security for network and server rooms

Division 04

Cyber Security

Network visibility Threat detection Professional services

Our newest division, deliberately narrow. We represent two specialist principals — NEOX Networks for network visibility infrastructure and Stamus Networks for network-based threat detection and response — and we deliver both with professional services rather than shipping appliances and walking away.

That means design of the capture and aggregation layer, deployment and tuning of detection, integration with the tools your SOC already runs, and knowledge transfer to the team who will live with it.

  • Network TAPs, packet brokers and traffic aggregation design
  • Network detection and response deployment and tuning
  • Full-packet visibility for SOC, forensics and OT environments
  • Integration with existing SIEM and monitoring stacks
  • Proof of concept, commissioning and operator handover
